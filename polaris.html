Polaris Browser

The browser,
complete.

Fast, quiet, and free. Polaris keeps ads and trackers out on its own, helps you find the page you read last week, and gets out of the way of everything else.

Download Polaris Pricing

Free, with nothing held back. For Windows 10 and 11.

Less browser.
More of the page.

Ads and trackers, kept out

Blocking is built in, so there is nothing to install. Turn it down for any site that needs it, and see what was kept away.

Find what you read

Polaris remembers the pages you actually read, not the ones you bounced off, so you can search for them later by what was on them.

A glance before you go

Peek at a link without leaving the page you are on. Keep it as a tab only if it was worth it.

Sites work as they should

Polaris is built on Chromium, the engine under Chrome and Edge, so the web looks and behaves the way it was made to.

Your sign-ins, if you want

Sign in with your Nomad account and the sites you are signed in to come with you to your next computer. Or don't; Polaris works fully without an account.

Calm by design

One slim bar, tabs in a sidebar, and nothing on the screen that is not the page or something you asked for.

Polaris in AX

Already on your desk.

Polaris is the browser inside every AX desk. Each desk keeps its own sign-ins, so your work accounts stay on your work desk.
