Get Nomad's apps.

Everything here runs on Windows 10 and 11. Each app has a build for Intel and AMD PCs and one for ARM; we point out the one that fits this computer.

AX

The desktop that lives in your account.

Needs an AX plan. AX Starter is free for 14 days.

AX for Intel or AMDWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit AX for ARMSnapdragon and other ARM64 PCs

Polaris Browser

The browser, complete.

Free. A Nomad account is optional.

Polaris for Intel or AMDWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit Polaris for ARMSnapdragon and other ARM64 PCs

Reach

Mail, chat and calls, together.

Free with a Nomad account. Reach Premium adds more.

Reach for Intel or AMDWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit Reach for ARMSnapdragon and other ARM64 PCs

Drift

The Drift app, for sending straight to your devices.

Needs Drift Premium, AX Pro or AX Ultra. To send a link, no download: drift.nomad.ax.

Drift for Intel or AMDWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit Drift for ARMSnapdragon and other ARM64 PCs

Nomad Office

Folio, Grids, Expo and Markup.

Needs Nomad Office or any AX plan.

Nomad Office for Intel or AMDWindows 10 and 11, 64-bit Nomad Office for ARMSnapdragon and other ARM64 PCs

The apps install for your Windows user alone, so they need no administrator, and they uninstall from Windows' own list of apps. Until our builds are code-signed, Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown. Not sure which PC you have? Intel and AMD is the usual answer; ARM means a Snapdragon or similar chip.
