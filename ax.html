Questions.

What is a desk?

A complete workspace: its own apps, files, windows and browser sign-ins. Most people need one. AX Pro gives you three, so work and home never meet; AX Ultra gives you eight.

What happens when I use another computer?

Install AX, sign in with your Nomad account, and your desk opens the way you left it. Your files and settings live in your account, not on the machine.

How does the free trial work?

AX Starter is free for 14 days. You start it, and later change or cancel it, at billing.nomad.ax. Nothing is ever paid inside the app.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Moving up or down is priced on the spot, with credit for what is left of your current plan. If you already pay for Nomad Office, Reach Premium or Drift Premium on their own, an AX plan that includes them replaces that payment.

Do I need a Google or Microsoft account?

No. A Nomad account is your email and a passkey or password. If you want your Gmail, Outlook or Google Drive in AX, you connect them inside the apps.

Which computers does AX run on?

Windows 10 and 11 PCs, with Intel, AMD or ARM processors. The download page picks the right one for you.
