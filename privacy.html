Privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Nomad is a desktop that lives in your account. To do that it keeps your files, your spaces and your settings on our servers, so they are there when you sign in on another machine. This page says what we keep, why, who helps us keep it, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Nomad is run by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME, REGISTERED ADDRESS]. For anything on this page, write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address, and how you sign in (a password we never see in plain text, or your Google or Microsoft sign-in).
  • What you put in Nomad: your files, their earlier versions, your spaces, their layouts, snapshots, wallpapers, installed apps and settings.
  • Reach: your messages, contacts and meetings. If you connect Gmail or Outlook, we keep the access Google or Microsoft gives us so Reach can read and send mail for you.
  • Connected services: if you connect Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, we keep the access that service gives us and a copy of your files from it, so they appear in Nomad as a folder you can edit and stay in sync both ways. If you connect GitHub, we keep the access GitHub gives us and copy the folders you choose into private repositories in your own GitHub account.
  • Billing: your plan, payments and receipts. Card numbers go to our payment provider; we keep only the brand, the last four digits and the expiry.
  • This machine: some things stay on the computer and never reach us: clipboard history, the window positions on that screen, and the PIN you type (we keep only a protected form of it).

Why we keep it

To run Nomad for you: to bring your desk back on any machine, to sync it, to send what you ask Drift to send, to bill your plan, and to email you about your account (sign-in codes, receipts, PIN resets). We do not sell your data, we do not show you ads, and we do not use your files to train anything.

Google, Microsoft, Dropbox and GitHub data

When you connect Google Drive, Nomad reads, creates, changes and removes files in your Google Drive only to keep the Cloud › Google Drive folder in Nomad and your Google Drive the same. The same holds for OneDrive and Dropbox. Nomad never uses this data for advertising, never sells it, never lets people read it except when you ask us to help with your account or the law requires it, and never uses it to train any model. You can disconnect any of them at any time in Settings › Connections; disconnecting stops the sync and removes our access.

Nomad's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who helps us

  • Supabase stores the account and your files, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Cloudflare serves our website and carries Drift's download links.
  • MontyPay takes card payments.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Google and Microsoft, only if you choose to sign in with them, connect their mail, or connect Google Drive or OneDrive.
  • Dropbox, only if you connect it.
  • GitHub, only if you connect it, to keep the folders you choose in private repositories in your own account.

How long

As long as you have an account. Earlier versions of files are kept for as long as your plan's history allows. When you delete your account, everything above is erased from our servers, except payments and receipts, which tax law requires us to keep.

What you can do

  • See and take your data: Settings › Accounts › Export my data copies every file, and your account data as a file, to your computer.
  • Correct it: change your name in Settings › Accounts, and anything else you made, in Nomad itself.
  • Delete it: Settings › Accounts › Delete your Nomad account.
  • Ask us: write to the address above. If you are in the EU or UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in Nomad before it takes effect.
